about me —

I've spent my career innovating during moments of technological change.

From early web applications at NASA and visual effects for Academy Award-winning films to enterprise software, AI, and cybersecurity, I've consistently explored pushing possibilities at the intersection of emerging technology and human experience. While technology inherently fascinates me, I'm even more interested in how we leverage it to enhance human capabilities and potential.

I've designed products across AI, cybersecurity, healthcare, enterprise software, and fintech, translating complex systems into intuitive, human-centered experiences. Along the way I've built UX research practices, helped establish DesignOps organizations, led globally distributed design teams, and mentored the next generation of designers through higher education and industry programs.

Today, my work spans product design, AI consulting, and design education. Whether I'm designing enterprise software, facilitating product discovery, or teaching college-level design courses, I'm driven by the same goal: helping people confidently engage with and utilize emerging technologies.

Before product design became my primary focus, I worked in visual effects on films including The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King and The Chronicles of Riddick, and later completed an MFA in Fine Art Media. My artistic practice explores identity, language, and embodiment through digital media, installation, and immersive technologies. Although my studio practice is less active today, it continues to shape the way I approach design, encouraging curiosity, experimentation, and a deep appreciation for the human side of technology.

I've learned that the best products aren't simply functional or technically sophisticated. They free us up to do more human things.
