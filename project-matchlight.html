Matchlight —

Matchlight was a Digital Risk Protection (B2B SaaS) solution that monitored large and diverse data sets for customer asset exposure across the deep, dark and open web, while maintaining customer privacy with patented fingerprinting technology.

 

As the sole product designer, I was responsible for the entire product design process, from discovery to launch. Matchlight was acquired by Deloitte in 2021.
